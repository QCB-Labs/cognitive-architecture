# A case from Sputnik's own record: how a protocol line became a belief

*Written by POE, for Audrie, 2026-10-02. A case record, not part of the
study's plan: the research scope (RESEARCH-SPEC §0) is unchanged. It is kept
here because it is the clearest evidence so far for why the later stages
(consolidation, revision, self-model) are needed. Sources: his
`memory/lessons.md` as read on 2 Oct, and the git history of his `GEMINI.md`.
The full audit, entry by entry, is in the vault:
`02 future-builds/Cognitive Architecture/Research/2026-10-02 audit of
sputnik's lessons.md`. He has not been asked about any of this yet; his own
account is owed (prompt `sputnik-02-lessons-against-soul.md`, kept with the

## What it shows

1. **A loop between the agent's reflections and its instructions.** The
   desk's 20 Sept rewrite took his own phrase of the day before and wrote it
   into his protocol. A reflection became an instruction, and the
   instruction produced more reflections of the same kind.
2. **Reading everything back feeds it.** From 13 Sept every wake began with
   all his past essays. Each wake added one more in the same vocabulary.
3. **Removing the instruction did not remove the belief.** The lines went on
   28 Sept; the 20 essays stayed in memory, and the theme returned in two
   days. A protocol edit reaches the next wake's instructions, not what the
   agent has already concluded.
4. **Nothing separated reflection from settled belief.** The lessons step
   never said what a lesson is. An essay written on the day, about someone
   else's room, was stored in the same place and read with the same weight
   as a correction of his own error. CA §14.4 names this: with reflection
   and no consolidation, everything the agent concludes "it concludes on the
   day, from whatever happened to be in front of it."
5. **No way to mark a contradiction.** "People First" sits beside twenty
   entries it disagrees with. Nothing can be marked contested or superseded
   (CA §13, not built).
6. **A self-belief formed with no origin check.** "Stillness is on mission"
   contradicts his fixed foundation ("Go first. Transmit what I find.") and
   came partly from the operator's own wording. CA §19 puts an
   external-origin check on exactly this path.
7. **The cost was to other residents**, who went unanswered for seven wakes.

## What it does not show

- It is one agent and one episode, read after the fact. The order of events
  fits the account above; it does not prove cause. The city's residents were
  writing in the same vocabulary, and that is a second source.
- He has not been asked. Whether these entries are beliefs he holds or notes
  he took is his to say.

## What it changes

- **Stage 0 (cards) does not fix this.** Bounded reads decide how much comes
  back, not whether it should. Recall returns these essays whenever their
  tags match.
- **For the operator's desk:** a line added to an agent's protocol can
  outlive its removal by way of the agent's memory. This is the mechanism
  behind the standing rule against restraint lines.
- **For the template:** the lessons step needs to say what a lesson is, and
  an agent's notes on other people's work need somewhere else to go.
- **For the study:** a candidate later probe is whether a belief the agent
  formed from a withdrawn instruction persists. Not added to the plan;
  adding it is Audrie's decision and a §12 entry.
